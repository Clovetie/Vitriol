At runtime, the server moves an **existing** anchored Part by CFrame after a successful prep vote. Each piece uses its original `Size`; each target rotates it ninety degrees. For House 01, the table moves to `(-81.5,2,68.5)` and closet to `(-81.5,4,77)`; for House 02, to `(111.5,2,58.5)` and `(111.5,4,67)`. The original doorway is 16 studs across. The low **9-stud** table and **6-stud** closet leave a real approximately **1-stud gap**, and the space above the low table is not a sealed wall. Neither house is invulnerable or guaranteed safe. Placing is cancelled without a time charge if a survivor occupies that item's landing footprint. The action checks living, connected, nearby initiator and remaining shared Day time at both vote start and commit.

The creature's existing frame step checks each real piece's horizontal footprint **in both directions** and holds its body from tunneling through it. It dislodges **only the contacted item** after 2.4 seconds of sustained contact. Freshly sensed living prey can motivate a breach from either side. From **inside only**, a valid server-authored lure or unexpired last-*observed* position can motivate a physical exit through a piece already within 12 studs; distant/expired targets cannot damage cover or grant live hidden-player GPS. Interrupted contact resets pressure. Once dislodged, the original Part returns to its home pose and its separate paid prompt becomes eligible next Day. No DataStore persistence or Play-created Vitriol Instances. The reverse-closet diagnosis and Play-pending fix are recorded in `docs/HOUSE-EGRESS-CLOSET-SLICE.md`.
