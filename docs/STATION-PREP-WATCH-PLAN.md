## Agreed rules and implementation

- Actions: **take fuel (4 main seconds), power garage (12), prime church bell (8), board garage (20; user-set), repair station floodlight (16).** Other living nearby consenting players can save **1 s each, at most 2 s total** during a **3-real-second** helper window for every action, including fuel pickup. Net cost scales by `DayNightConfig.Pacing.DaySeconds / 210`, after discount, and caps at remaining Day time. Dawn charges the upcoming Day; Day charges jump once through TimeService's normal phase callbacks, never spill into Night.
- `PrepCoordinator` is server-only; it checks part range, registered player, living/non-permadead state, action-specific readiness at start, vote close and blackout. Distinct other UserIds must volunteer on the existing disabled-in-Edit helper prompts; helpers are revalidated at commit, and one transaction is active at a time. It fails closed if the fade transport is absent. Absolute `Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` deadlines keep the vote three real seconds even if Cadence drops a beat. It reuses DayPrep's existing 0.25-second Cadence slot—no extra heartbeat or task loop. A frame spike cannot broadcast blackout, charge and reveal all in one callback.
- TimeService adds an optional **ninth field** to preauthored `RE_TimeSync`: server-timestamped serialized stage (`vote`, `blackout`, `reveal`, `clear` or `cancel`). Repeat time snapshots retain the original start timestamp; late joiners receive the current stage, stale serials are ignored. `PrepFadeController` changes only the existing per-player `SharedFade` Frame, connects to RenderStepped only during an animation, and creates no UI, Camera, Tween, game object or new remote. `ClientMain` replays an early stage after binding the optional watch/fade; the original first-person wrist readout remains hold-T and uses authoritative Day budget.
- `FloodlightService` operates the preauthored switch `(-62,4.2,-110)`, fixture/`StationBeam` and helper. Day/Dawn repair once; Night beam reveals the filling-station route and emits periodic bounded EM/AIR hum. As a **weaker fallback** lure it draws a distant Braid only if neither the accepted church bell nor powered garage lure is active; nearby living prey within 32 studs still wins. Dawn resets the repair and extinguishes the beam. The user's relocated bell lever `(119,2.5,-210)`, bell audio/flashes, garage breach, planted-foot-led Braid gait/chomp and stored/unavailable barricade Tool are unchanged. Night garage emergency unboarding remains immediate and free.
- All authored helper and repair prompts remain **disabled in Edit mode** and are enabled only by validated server services during a fresh Play session. No game objects/UI are created or cloned at runtime.
