## Why this exists

The user Play-tested the new two-house furniture cover: **players can enter, but the Braid is too high, climbed onto a roof, and could not get down or enter**. Preserve the approved planted-foot-led front/back-bank gait, high-prey response and lower-then-charge chomp. Do not change the user's house geometry or flatten its roof.

A guarded read-only survey of BOTH actual Studio houses found the east doorway, porch roof underside **Y=12.5**, door lintel underside **Y=12**, main roof underside **Y≈16.3**, roof top **Y≈17.7**, and original floor top **Y≈0.8**. ## Implementation

- `Creature/HouseAccess.luau` reads the existing `Floor`, `EastPier_-13`, `EastLintel`, `Roof` and `PorchRoof` at each house. It derives a radius-aware clearance from **the actual underside** and routes already-sensed grounded prey through the real east entrance. An approach from behind skirts the exterior flank instead of crossing a wall. It never creates a Path, Part or runtime Instance.
- A Braid already atop the house/porch roof first travels to a waypoint **beyond the porch eave and core radius** at its current height; ONLY in that unobstructed exterior strip can it lower (bounded at 10 studs/s). From there it approaches under the porch at a body-center ceiling of `min(lintel bottom, porch underside) − CoreRadius − 0.5` (10 studs for a 1.5-stud radius), crosses the real doorway, and may resume its normal elevated stance under the higher interior roof. The pure route never moves or reshapes the house or instantly teleports a Braid through its roof.
- `Locomotion` retains its approved horizontal stance/step/haul. While *actually under a house's measured ceiling*, it rejects overhead rooftop footholds, refreshes stale high anchors, and limits **only vertical** motion. Elsewhere the original wall/ceiling purchase and height remain. `CreatureCore` additionally guards the two solid roof slabs from downward BulkMoveTo phasing if footholds are lost. The existing cover pieces still intercept/brace a Braid; when pursuing sensed prey deep inside a room, the Braid presses the furniture in its **actual doorway waypoint**, not a straight ray through the pier.
- Ground-level directional sensing formerly could acquire a player's live coordinates through a roof when the Braid was above them. A pure segment/slab test now blocks that particular roof-occluded sight/heat. The Braid may recover off the roof **without** knowing the hidden player's position, and high prey *on* the roof retains the accepted response. The bell/garage route and ordinary open-ground gait were not redesigned.
