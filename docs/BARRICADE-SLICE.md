## Game rule

During Day/Dawn, a living player within nine studs can hold the latch prompt for three seconds to board the bay. Boarding is server-validated, emits AIR and VIBRATION noise, spends the one preauthored plank set for that night, and makes the barrier visible, solid and queryable. Duplicate/competing activations cannot build extra boards. During Night, a pursuing Braid at the bay cannot simply move through the anchored gate or start a chomp through it. Pressure builds when a core step attempts to cross the bay plane, when the body is within five studs with a chase target on the other side through the opening, **or when a tendril's existing raycast foothold is the exact authored gate even though the body remains farther back**. An unrelated floor/wall foothold cannot trigger a breach. Sustained pressure breaches it in roughly 2.4 seconds with a larger noise impulse; the usual chase then resumes. This buys time, not invulnerability. A survivor can use the same latch to **throw the gate open** at any phase, including Night, to escape; this is loud and consumes the boards rather than allowing immediate rebuild. At Dawn, the authored barrier returns to an invisible/nonblocking state and its single build opportunity resets. No Part, Prompt, light, UI or remote is created during Play—only authored properties and service state change.

**Known scope:** Pressure requires a through-bay opposite-side target **and** proximity to the gate or an actual gate foothold. A distant target alone, a same-side target, or an unrelated support contact cannot accrue pressure. The bounded garage detour in `docs/GARAGE-NAVIGATION.md` does not provide town-wide pathfinding or wall destruction. The gate remains blockout art; actual body/tendril bracing and breach timing still need a human Play verdict. The player can abandon the boarded bay via the inside latch.
