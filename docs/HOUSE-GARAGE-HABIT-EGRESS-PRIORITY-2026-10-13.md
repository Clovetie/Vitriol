## The regression and narrow correction

`CreatureService:_search` previously offered an already-witnessed Garage habit its physical approach ray *before* checking whether a clue-less Braid was grounded inside a house. An optimistic clear Garage ray could therefore take priority over the indoor, lower-first House doorway sweep after the local circuit, even though the Braid still needed to get out through its actual door. A new House fixture with a genuinely thresholded Garage habit and a ray stub deliberately accepting that outdoor lead reproduced the conflict **before** the change: the indoor doorway-priority assertion failed. This is a synthetic priority counterexample, **not** a claim it happened in human Play. Real authored walls may reject the Garage ray; that would still waste a Think and can delay the required House exit.

Only `code/ServerStorage/Vitriol/Services/CreatureService.luau` changed. On a clue-less grounded House search, the service now suspends (does not erase) any cached Garage route throughout the local patrol. After **one completed** local circuit it performs the existing lower-first, bounded House exit sweep ahead of Garage habit, including safe no-ray lowering, staging and the at-most-one-new-candidate-ray-per-Think limit. If no body-width exit exists, it continues local patrol rather than spending a competing Garage ray. A follow-up seam regression found that `groundHouse()` ends as soon as the **core center** reaches the east opening, about a core radius before the full body is clear. An otherwise valid Garage preference could reclaim the target in that narrow band. The local fix now keeps the *already verified* outward goal and exit-only supported posture until the full core clears the authored pier face, using **zero additional candidate rays** while traversing this seam. A three-second no-progress watchdog aborts that held leg; the stalled core resumes local search and blocks an unsafe Garage retry for the current Night without deleting witnessed evidence. Once the full body is outside, the same run-local Garage evidence may choose its front approach again, but must spend a **fresh** physical safety ray. Fresh genuine prey, bell/light/noise and recent real clues still have their established higher priority in `think()`; the change adds no hidden-player location, Instance, extra budget, map edit, Q-learning or Day/Dawn behavior.
